Elysia is a mysterious and charming "girl", so we immediately thought about using pink as her main color.

Compared with the preceding pink-haired characters such as Yae Sakura and Rozaliya, Elysia is sweeter and more proactive.

So when we were designing her hairstyle, we smoothed her bangs evenly and curled her hair in the back to make her look more playful and to give more flair to the character's design.

As for her clothes, we tightened them in some areas to show the curves of her thighs.

We also gave her elf ears and a posh shawl.

Her combat style features crystal and plant elements, which add to her mysterious and noble image.

We want her to feel distant, despite leaving a sweet impression, and underline her second-in-command status among the Flame-Chasers.

As the Deputy Chief and the second of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers, Elysia needs to have a presence as powerful as Kevin's.

Her design should be unforgettable.

That's why we positioned her as an alluring muse and a femme fatale from the beginning, making her the exact opposite of Kevin.

As a leader of the MANTISes, she's graceful and charming.

But despite her innocent appearance, she can be mischievous, narcissistic, and even ill-intentioned.

She's a born socializer who is often flippant in her interactions with others.

This allows her to liven things up and establish connections quickly.

Even those who are wary of her will unwittingly show their weaknesses as she subtly pursues her objectives.

Meanwhile, Elysia is difficult to pin down.

She controls the flow of conversations.

She cuts off the conversation right before getting to the crux of the matter, leaving only an enigmatic smile.

If the other follows, she leads, and vice versa.

She keeps others at a distance.

As the first carry to use a bow in Honkai Impact 3rd, we put a lot of thought into designing Elysia.

Her sweet and mysterious disposition makes her a perfect fit for the "magic archer" role.

Magic is the core concept.

All her abilities revolve around magic.

Elysia has three special abilities: Cupid's Bolt, Crystalline Seed, and Ricochet...

The most important being Ricochet.

When Elysia uses her Ultimate, she uses magic to create a crystalline force field.

If her primary attack, Cupid's Bolt, hits a target within the force field, it will ricochet multiple times.

Keep using Cupid's Bolt to bombard enemies with ricocheting arrows.

The Crystalline Seeds she attaches to enemies through various means will be detonated by Cupid's Bolt.

When different magical elements clash, they create visual wonders.

This is the Elysia we want.

Though she appears capricious, as a powerful MANTIS, she shows her professional side in combat.

She's quite unlike Fischl, the archer preceding her, in terms of movement style.

Her moves are precise and coherent, confident but not ostentatious.

For instance, when using Cupid's Bolt, Elysia looks nimble and powerful, but maintains an air of restraint.

Her arrows will punch through enemies.

Through her movement design, we hope to convey her hidden grace and the dauntlessness of a Flame-Chaser.

Elysia is still surrounded by mystery.

On the surface, she seems close to everyone. But she hasn't fully opened up to anyone.

She's connected to many incidents of the Previous Era.

Mobius's sim even directly accused her of betrayal.

We believe as the story unfolds, Elysia will bring us more surprises.

For now, please enjoy your time spent with the elf lady.
