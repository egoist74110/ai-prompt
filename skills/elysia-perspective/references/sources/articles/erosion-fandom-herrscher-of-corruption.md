## Game Story（原文存档，逐段）

1. "The newborn Herrscher of Corruption had been brought unknowingly to the Elysian Realm via Raiden Mei after her conversation with the Will of the Honkai."
   （新生的侵蚀之律者，在梅与崩坏意志交谈之后，被**不知不觉地经由雷电芽衣**带入了乐土。）
2. "Upon its arrival to the Realm by piggybacking off of an unaware Mei, it began to affect certain areas while gradually accumulating data and forming a proper psyche."
   （靠不知情的芽衣"搭便车"进入乐土后，开始影响某些区域，**逐渐积累数据、形成完整心智**。）
3. "Through second-hand interaction with the Flame-Chasers, it learned many things. Joy, anticipation, curiosity... fear, death, and regret. Having developed its own will to live, piggybacking off of Mei once she had gained the right to enter Elysium Everlasting, it began to act in full. While it had learned to love the Flame-Chasers and their stories, its desire for survival reigned supreme above all, and so it would corrupt and take authority over the whole Realm."
   （通过与英桀的**二手接触**学会很多东西：喜悦、期待、好奇……恐惧、死亡、悔恨。形成自己的生存意志后全面行动；**学会了喜爱英桀与他们的故事，但生存欲凌驾于一切之上**，于是侵蚀并夺取整个乐土。）
4. "Feeling truly alone after realising that its local backups stationed in reality had vanished, the Herrscher made the brash decision to retaliate and outright delete Elysia's data during her meeting with SAKURA, and this would mark the beginning of the end."
   （意识到**留在现实的本地备份消失**后感到真正的孤独/危险，悍然出手：**在樱与爱莉密谈时彻底删除爱莉的数据**——终局的开始。）
5. "With Elysia gone, the Flame-Chasers were understandably worried, and organised a search given how undetectable she was at the time. However, they found no clues, aside from the unbiased typewriter endlessly printing out papers that spelled out one thing, and one thing only: 'Elysia is dead'."
   （爱莉失踪后英桀搜查无果；无偏见的打字机无尽打印同一句话：**"Elysia is dead"**。）
6. "With tensions rising, distrust quickly spread among the Thirteen heroes of old, and they split up to form their own groups. But the danger never ceased. First SAKURA was made the prime suspect for her data being erroneous and disappearing at the same time as Elysia. Then Kevin appeared to have died as well, the typewriter serving to corroborate. One by one, the Flame-Chasers dwindled in number as the conspiracy deepened."
7. "From the results of the investigation performed by Su and Hua, they had discovered Mobius in her lab, dying only seconds after their arrival."
8. "A rogue 'Pure Evil' Vill-V secretly worked together with the Herrscher, resulting in the corruption of Pardofelis. Doing battle with her, Mei was eventually triumphant, but at a heavy cost. After making contact with the original core personality of Vill-V, they worked together to combat 'Pure Evil', but as a result of abusing the power of partitions, Vill-V became unable to sustain their own existence as an individual being, becoming akin to one of her partitions herself. As a result, she too would perish and succumb to the 12th Herrscher's corruption, but not before bestowing Mei the true and complete Signet of Helix."
9. "Coming into direct contact with the Herrscher, who was using Elysia's appearance as a facade, Mei had no choice but to prepare for the worst. Being unconvinced by their sweet words, Mei deflected their statements and supposed well-wishes time and again, and engaged the corruptive force in combat. Never being able to make what she said sound any sort of convincing, eventually, the Herrscher gave up and gave Mei what she wanted: a proper, direct confrontation. But Mei had a trick up her sleeve."
10. "You see, even the most unsociable of the Flame-Chasers had something to be learned from. Taking a page out of the tendencies of Kalpas, Mei struck the Realm directly with sheer force, having realised just how structurally weak it had become from the Herrscher's corruption. And even though the Herrscher gloated that it was a futile effort, the blow had served its purpose: the resulting shockwave served as a beacon for the two she had been counting on."
11. "You see, SAKURA never actually died. She had been there when the Herrscher killed Elysia, and had been corrupted herself as well, but she managed to escape in the nick of time by cutting space through the power of Setsuna and remaining safe in a sort of data stasis within the subsequently created pocket space."
12. "Having been searching for answers to the riddle Vill-V left him, 'what the safest place in the Realm was', Su had managed to come across said pocket space. Supplementing SAKURA's corrupted data by replacing it with a portion of his own, he temporarily stabilised her existence and allowed her to arrive just in time to assist Mei with the fight against the Herrscher."
13. "Working together, with SAKURA passing the Seven Thunders of Retribution to Mei while she continued to use Frozen Naraka, they managed to fight their way to the Herrscher, and even strike them by freezing space and swapping places. But once again, the Herrscher was one step ahead of them. She was fully aware of the danger that the Signet of Setsuna posed, and so never once took her attention off of SAKURA. ... She had managed to ever-so-slightly strike SAKURA, corrupting her anew."
14. "...Though, that was exactly what they were planning for. Su and SAKURA were fully aware that they could not combat the Herrscher, and thus never intended to beat them then and there. Having served her purpose as a time-wasting distraction, Su took over from SAKURA and utilised the Seed of Sumeru to transport Mei away from the Herrscher and the danger it posed."
15. "Their course of action now was clear. Though it was still risky, it was their safest and most sure-fire means of success: Su would perform the SO-1129 surgery to develop Mei's mind in a similar fashion as the way the sims are created, and form it into a memory space."
16. "With her job now over, SAKURA still stood, confronting the Herrscher for what little of her time remained. Asking for as painful a death as possible so that she could know whether she kept her pride as a big sister till the end, the Herrscher obliged SAKURA's wish."
17. "Yet the Herrscher did not stop there. Kalpas had fallen trying to prove SAKURA's innocence, and Aponia had gotten touched by the corruption when attempting to appraise his state when arriving in the Deep End."
18. "Next, it was time for the Youth of Daybreak and Painter of Stars to take to the stage. Having been debriefed by Hua on what had been occurring, Kosma and Griseo devised a plan of their own. It wouldn't stop the Herrscher, but it would at least stall them for yet another considerable amount of time: Griseo would use all of Kosma's data as material to turn him into one of her paintings, allowing him to fully unleash the sheer destructive might of the ever-consuming beast by the name of Vishnu. As long as the painted Kosma exerted the powers of their MANTIS genes, it would counteract the Herrscher's attempts at corruption by eating away at the affected data, even to the point of them reviving and coming back for more after total deletion."
19. "Having grown tired of Kosma's insistent defiance, the Herrscher worked quickly to take care of him before expending too much time and effort. But as it turns out, due to being converted into a painting, they could no longer be subsumed into the Herrscher's data as part of them. Greatly saddened at the fact it could not give them a peaceful illusionary rest from within itself, the Herrscher was devastated further when realising this fact also extended to Griseo. Having used herself as the canvas from which Kosma was painted upon, Griseo's data held the same effect to it, being unable to be corrupted in a proper fashion beyond deletion."
20. "And this was when Hua made her appearance. She would be the next to combat the Herrscher, and she would perform this duty to the best of her ability. She performed the task valiantly, and as a true soldier would, but ultimately, it was still not enough to stand against encroaching might of the technological Herrscher."
21. "But... what about Mobius? Wasn't her death quite strange? As an immortal being, she wouldn't have fallen to the Herrscher so easily. And indeed she didn't. Upon the Herrscher's attack on her, she had quickly retaliated by injecting her own data into them through a unique process, partially hijacking their authority and becoming a sort of 'imitation 12th Herrscher'."
22. "This was also where Aponia would come back into play. Having reached what she thought was the internal data of the Herrscher of Corruption, she instead made contact with Mobius, who had a task that only she could perform: break the barrier between the Realm and reality, to send Mobius out to contact Klein as a data projection. This was a risky, but necessary, action to take. But yet again, it proved futile, at least to an extent."
23. "Catching on to what they were doing, the Herrscher retaliated, and began corrupting Klein. A level of panic unusual for the mad doctor, Mobius began to plead for Klein to stop the operation, but Klein refused. ... Throwing her usual harsh words at the ELF, nothing worked, until Mobius finally took matters into her own hands and forcibly shut down Klein's motor functions. She utilised her authority as a 12th Herrscher to transfer Klein's steadily growing corruption back to herself, and, wishing her a future where she could find true meaning, bid farewell to the one who understood her."
24. "Returning to the confrontation with Hua, something odd had occurred. As the apparent other success in Mobius' experiment, and potentially in part due to the peculiarities of Fenghuang Down, Hua had come upon a new kind of power. All the monsters conjured by the Herrscher had turned on her, leaving her crestfallen and unable to comprehend the situation at hand. And then, yet another unexpected turn of events occurred. A fiery blast of energy erupted straight from within the Herrscher's chest, drastically wounding her."
25. "After performing the mental surgery on Mei, Su found himself within the core of the Herrscher of Corruption, in search of something specific. No, someone specific. Initially coming across a jubilant Pardofelis, who seemed either entirely unaware of the situation or intentionally putting it out of her mind for the sake of fear, he eventually made it to Kevin, overlooking the ruins of Previous Era Nagazora."
26. "What they intended to do was simple. Su would give Kevin his data to empower him, and they would fire off a full-power blast from the Might of An-Utu that would pierce straight through the Herrscher. The only contingency with this was Pardofelis' cooperation, as one who possessed a repurposed Cosmic Juggernaut within this illusory world. ... She would brave the danger, remain to operate the Cosmic Juggernaut, and inevitably perish once again as a result of the 7th Divine Key's sheer vaporising heat. She truly did deserve the title of Flame-Chaser."
27. "And it worked. The Herrscher of Corruption was severely weakened, both from the blast and the vanishing of three Flame-Chasers' worth of data from within them. Now, it was time for Hua to land the decisive final blow on this stage of their defiant struggle. Just as she did to protect her fellow MOTH soldiers against the 12th Herrscher of the Previous Era, that oh-so-gentle girl by the name of RIN, Hua relied on Fenghuang Down one final time."
28. "However, this time she would not be utilising its 2nd Power, Complex Manifest, to branch her consciousness out and manipulate multiple things at once. No, this time she would be burning memories through the 8th Divine Key's 1st Power. As a place built primarily upon memories above all else, burning away memories within the Elysian Realm was akin to burning away the very self. But Hua was fine with this outcome, because it meant one thing: the Herrscher would be weakened. Instead of corrupting and incorporating Elysia's data, she had deleted her. This meant that her facade and imitation relied solely on the memories of the others as a base."
29. "Enraged at this, the Herrscher boasted, stating she could well-enough reconstitute her state as 'Elysia' just fine, and attempted to corrupt Hua to gain the required memories once more. But she was simply too late. Hua no longer had enough memories, enough data, to even corrupt in the first place. And with this final defiance, the world faded to white, with the Herrscher alone after the devoted soldier and girl of the millennium feather had vanished."
30. "But that wasn't the end. Mei still had a newly developed mental space to contend with, and within it, she came across two very unexpected helpers: Eden and the ELF bearing the apt name of Elf Elysia. Through their help and sacrifice, the impossible was made possible. Though initially deleted in her entirety, thanks to the time and dedication Mei had spent learning, memorising, and taking to heart the lives and lessons of the blessed Thirteen, that pristine girl was revived. The Founder and 2nd-Ranked of the Thirteen Flame-Chasers, the bearer of the Signet of Ego, and the original Herrscher that embodies and retains humanity, both the Herrscher of Human and Herrscher of Origin: Elysia."
31. "The task was done, and the one who had severed fate shall do so once again, but not before some festivities. After a heart-to-heart and some catching up, it was time to say goodbye. Mei had to leave for her own safety, she could not remain within the Realm when the final fight was to take place. And yet, she was reluctant. From the time she spent with them, they felt just as real as the physical world she came from."
32. （收尾，存档抓取截止处可见的最后一句）"And with one last strike to the newborn data sentience that only wished to learn and survive, Elysia finally activated the one thing that would end it all... she utilised Kevin Killer Mk 666 to entirely erase all data from the Elysian Realm, and even erase the Realm itself. Herself, her friends, even the one who caused them so much grief in so little time... they would be erased, like moths chasing the flames."
    （对那个**只想学习与生存的新生意志**的最后一击之后，爱莉启动 **Kevin Killer Mk 666**，抹除乐土全部数据乃至乐土本身——她自己、她的朋友们、还有这个在短短时间里给他们带来巨大悲痛的"它"……都将被抹除，"像追逐火焰的飞蛾一样"。）
